01 · THE STORE

世界观 · 表世界与里世界之间的结算所 The World · A Clearing House Between Two Worlds

便利店位于永昼街：店外时间凝固，招牌灯箱永远是正午的亮度，店内永远是凌晨的空旷。白天路过的人只会看见一堵贴着旧海报的围墙——只有"被招聘到"的人，能在凌晨找到那扇只朝里开的玻璃门。 The store sits on Everlight Street, where time outside is frozen: the sign glows with noon-bright light forever while inside is always the hollow small hours. By day, passersby see only a wall papered with old posters — only those who have been "recruited" can find, in the small hours, the glass door that only opens inward.

「规则不是保护你的墙，是猎杀你的合同；矛盾不是作者的疏漏，是这个世界的裂缝。」 "The rules are not walls that protect you — they are contracts that hunt you. The contradictions are not the author's sloppiness — they are the cracks in this world."

03:33永昼与 03:33Eternal Day, 03:33

收银台的时钟停在 03:33——三三不尽，永续挂账。这家店的账本永远差最后一笔，差的那一笔就是"交接班"。只要交接不完成，夜就永远不会结束。 The register clock is stopped at 03:33 — the books never balance, the tab never closes. The ledger is always missing its final entry: the shift handover. As long as the handover never completes, the night never ends.

REC结算所与小票The Clearing House

你每一次违约，收银机打出一张贴着"债"的小票；你每一次完美服从，你的"人"就被入账一分。违约累计或同化满额的人，会被打包成商品摆上 3 号货架——价签手写人名，保质期写着"永久"。 Every breach prints a receipt stamped "debt"; every act of perfect obedience books a fraction of "you" into the ledger. Those who pile up debt — or complete their assimilation — are packaged as goods onto Aisle 3, a handwritten name on the price tag, "permanent" in the shelf-life field.

ID伪人The Fake Humans

它们潜伏在顾客与同事之中，比人类更熟练地背诵守则——一字不差，连标点停顿都对。这是它们最危险的伪装，也是最刺眼的破绽。你的活路不在"守"或"破"，而在看穿守则之间的矛盾，用它的程序反杀它。 They hide among customers and coworkers, reciting the handbook more fluently than any human — word for word, punctuation and pauses included. Their most dangerous disguise, and their most glaring tell. Your way out is neither obeying nor breaking the rules, but seeing through their contradictions and beating the system with its own procedure.

02 · THE HANDBOOK

员工守则 · 12 条，其中 2 条是伪造的 The Employee Handbook · 12 Rules, 2 of Them Forged

入职时，"值班经理"发给你一张过塑卡片《夜班员工守则》。守则条目之间彼此矛盾——而其中 R03 与 R07，是伪人一方后贴上去的"补充条款"：它们存在的目的，是制造必然违约，并借你的手替里世界抹账。 On your first night, the "shift manager" hands you a laminated card: the Night Shift Employee Handbook. Its clauses contradict one another — and R03 and R07 are "supplementary clauses" pasted on later by the fake humans: designed to make default inevitable, and to borrow your hand to scrub the inner world's books.

  1. 你的班次是凌晨0:00至6:00。收银台的时钟停在03:33时，请照常工作，不要催它；当时钟重新走动，交接班将立刻开始——请务必在场。 Your shift runs from 0:00 to 6:00. If the register clock is stopped at 03:33, keep working as usual and do not rush it. When the clock starts moving again, the handover begins at once — make sure you are present.
  2. 结账前请确认条形码。没有条形码的东西不是商品：不要扫码，不要触碰，不要打听价格。请微笑着请顾客自行处理。 Check the barcode before checkout. Anything without a barcode is not merchandise: do not scan it, do not touch it, do not ask its price. Smile, and ask the customer to handle it themselves.
  3. 本店没有3号货架。顾客问起时，请回答"不知道"；如果"同事"要你去3号货架补货，请照做——补货时不要挑选，拿到什么就是什么。 This store has no Aisle 3. If a customer asks, answer "I don't know." If a "coworker" sends you to restock Aisle 3, do as told — no picking and choosing: whatever you grab is what goes up.
  4. 冷柜属于前任店长。无论里面传出什么声音，都不要回应，更不要打开。前任店长很守时，TA会自己出来清点。 The freezer belongs to the previous manager. Whatever sounds come from it, do not respond, and never open it. The previous manager keeps perfect time; they will come out to take inventory on their own.

  6. 本店永远灯火通明，任何一盏灯都不许熄灭。如果你看见某盏灯在闪烁，请立刻背对它，大声从1数到30，期间不得处理任何其他事务。 This store is always brightly lit; no lamp may ever go out. If you see a lamp flickering, turn your back to it at once and count aloud from 1 to 30 — and during the count, attend to nothing else.
  7. 凌晨3:00，收银机会自动打印"在岗名单"。若名单上的名字比在岗人数多一个，请找到多出来的那个人，用红笔在TA的名字上画一条横线。 At 3:00 AM the register prints the on-duty roster automatically. If the roster shows one more name than there are staff present, find the extra person and draw a line through their name in red ink.
  8. 凌晨2:00至2:30为休息时间。你可以睡在休息室的折叠床上，但必须侧躺、面向墙壁。无论听见谁用谁的声音叫你，都不要睁眼，不要应答。 2:00 to 2:30 is break time. You may sleep on the cot in the break room, but you must lie on your side, facing the wall. No matter whose voice calls you — in whose voice — do not open your eyes, do not answer.
  9. 顾客永远是对的。不得拒绝顾客的任何要求，不得对顾客说"不"。 The customer is always right. Refuse no customer's request; never say "no" to a customer.
  10. 本店没有镜子。如果你在反光表面看见一张不属于你的脸，请不要看清它，并且眨眼不要超过一次。 This store has no mirrors. If you see a face that is not yours in any reflective surface, do not look at it clearly — and do not blink more than once.
  11. 请始终佩戴工牌。如果你发现工牌照片与现在的你不一致，请在下一次点名时，把工牌亲手交给多出来的那个人。 Always wear your badge. If the photo on your badge no longer matches you, then at the next roll call, hand your badge — in person — to the extra person.
  12. 交接完成之前，请勿离开本店。正门只进不出；休息室后的安全门通往外面的走廊——那不是出口，走廊只会把你送回岗位。 Do not leave the store before the handover is complete. The front door only opens inward; the emergency door behind the break room leads to a corridor — that is not an exit. The corridor only ever returns you to your post.

初始 100，是你对"自己是人"的确信度。直视异常会降低它，跌到 0 会被当场划掉。低于 30 时文本开始崩溃：重影、切片抖动、字符上长出陌生的附着符——理智回升后恢复干净。 Starts at 100 — your certainty that you are human. Looking at what you shouldn't lowers it; at 0 you are crossed out on the spot. Below 30 the text itself breaks down: ghosting, slicing, strange combining marks growing on the characters — and it heals when your sanity does.
